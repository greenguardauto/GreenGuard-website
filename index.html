Technology

Precision at Every Level

GreenGuard is pioneering deep tech in agriculture by developing a fully autonomous, laser-based weed control system. Our integrated platform merges cutting-edge computer vision, precision robotics, and advanced laser optics to deliver chemical-free solutions at scale.

Laser-Based Weed Control

High-efficiency lasers deliver precise energy to destroy weeds at the growth point — without chemicals or soil disturbance.
